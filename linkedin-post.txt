As more creative work moves into 3D, jewelry remains a demanding test of what automation can do.

Around six months ago, my team and I built a workflow that generated 3D rings. Today, we have extended that work to native Rhino CAD.

That progression has been my toughest engineering challenge so far.

Recently, I have also worked on automation pipelines for J., Sitara, Kollective Moda, and other garment businesses. Each project has taught me how much product details matter. Jewelry takes that lesson into a different kind of precision.

A ring is small, but its geometry is unforgiving. Proportions, curves, stone placement, and how the parts connect all need to work together. Bringing procedural designs into editable CAD pushed our work further.

I am proud of what my team and I have built, and I wanted to document this milestone under my name: abdullahanxie.
