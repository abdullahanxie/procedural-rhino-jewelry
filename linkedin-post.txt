Image or text to native Rhino jewelry CAD.

A jewelry reference describes appearance. A usable CAD model needs three-dimensional geometry that can be inspected, edited, and reviewed for the intended production process.

Our workflow addresses that conversion for supported ring designs, accepting an image or a text description and producing native Rhino .3dm geometry alongside a .glb preview.

The workflow is production-ready for supported ring designs, with CAD review before manufacture. That scope matters: incomplete references, unusual designs, stone fit, tolerances, and manufacturing requirements still need appropriate review.

Around six months ago, my team and I developed a workflow that generated 3D rings. We have since extended that work to native Rhino CAD. This sits alongside our automation work for J., Sitara, Kollective Moda, and other garment businesses.

The article explains the problem, output, production scope, and limitations. The diagram shows a simplified functional pipeline; internal methods remain private.
